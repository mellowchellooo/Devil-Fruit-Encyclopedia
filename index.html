One Piece is a manga series by Eiichiro Oda, running since 1997. Set in a world of pirates, the story follows Monkey D. Luffy and his crew as they search for the legendary treasure known as the "One Piece" — and Luffy's dream of becoming King of the Pirates.

Central to the world are Devil Fruits — mysterious fruits found across the oceans. Each grants a unique supernatural power to whoever eats it. The price: the eater loses their ability to swim forever. There are three known types — Logia, Zoan, and Paramecia — and no two fruits share the same power.

This encyclopedia documents every named Devil Fruit confirmed in the manga — including their abilities, known users, the chapter they were first revealed, and any known awakening details.
